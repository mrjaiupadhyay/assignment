Two Paths, One Spell

Elena Marlowe

Keeper of old stories and older recipes. She collects secondhand spellbooks she never quite finishes and can name every constellation from her grandmother's rooftop.

Theodore Ashworth

Builder of small wooden things and terrible puns. He talks to the crows in his garden and insists, quite seriously, that they talk back.

They met on a rainy Tuesday, in the poetry aisle of a shop that no longer exists — both reaching for the same worn copy of a book neither of them ever bought.
